Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Unpacked Stack ("we", "us") makes short educational videos about IT and software. This policy covers our publishing tool, Unpacked Stack Publisher, a private application we use to upload our own videos to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the Unpacked Stack team to publish content to accounts we own. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information of our own account (display name, avatar, open ID) and permission to upload videos to it.
  • YouTube: our own channel information (name, subscriber and video counts) and permission to upload videos to it.

We do not access, collect or process data about any other person, including viewers of our videos.

How the data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a private folder that is never shared or uploaded. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

How long we keep it

Tokens are kept until we disconnect the account in the tool or revoke access in the TikTok or Google account settings, at which point they are deleted.

Your choices

Because the tool only connects to our own accounts, no action is needed from viewers. If you have a question about this policy, contact us at the address below.
